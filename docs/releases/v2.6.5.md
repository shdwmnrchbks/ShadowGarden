# Shadow Garden v2.6.5 — Continuous Full-Bleed Canvas

Shadow Garden v2.6.5 corrects the visual imbalance introduced by the v2.6.4 canvas exclusion for reopened issue #160 item 2: keeping body side padding inside a structurally rail-excluded canvas left full-page artwork with a narrow margin on the left but roughly double that dead space on the right, which read as an even wider seek-rail bar covering more of the image.

## Changed

- Continuous publication bodies drop horizontal padding (`2.5em 0`), so media can bleed across the entire reading canvas and end exactly at the seek-rail boundary — matching webtoon/manga-style full-page strips.
- Readable prose insets moved to text selectors only (`p`, `li`, `dd`, `dt`, `blockquote`, `figcaption` receive 14px inline padding), so paragraph measure stays comfortable without ever indenting artwork twice.
- The v2.6.1–v2.6.3 containment caps and the v2.6.4 structural `.viewer` rail exclusion remain unchanged; `max-width: 100%` now resolves against a zero-padded body, so `100%` means "all the way to the rail start".

## Regression coverage

The five-shape mobile regression still requires every oversized artwork's rendered right edge to sit at or left of the fixed Continuous seek rail; with zero horizontal padding the dark full-bleed plate shape now spans from the screen's left edge to the rail boundary exactly, guarding both bleed and containment at once. Reconciliation guards pin the zero-horizontal-padding contract and the prose-only inset selectors.

The complete v2.6 Chromium, Firefox, WebKit, Chromium Mobile, and WebKit Mobile regression matrix remains required, together with the reusable v2 publisher production gates.
