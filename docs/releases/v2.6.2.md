# Shadow Garden v2.6.2 — Continuous Media Containment Hotfix

Shadow Garden v2.6.2 is a focused follow-up to the v2.6.1 rail hotfix for the reopened issue #160 item: oversized publication artwork still clipped at the right edge in Continuous mode even though the seek-rail overlap itself was fixed.

## Fixed

- Keep every common publication wrapper — `div`, `section`, `article`, `aside`, `main`, and `table` — bounded by the padded Continuous content box, not only `figure`/`picture`. Real books frequently wrap pages or panels in plain wrappers with fixed widths, and each uncapped ancestor kept feeding oversized boxes to nested media.
- Cap inline vector/replaced media (`svg`, `video`, `canvas`, `object`, `embed`) against the same content box so full-page SVG publications obey the rail boundary.
- Replace the previous `min(100%, 92vw)` image fallback with the exact content box. The vw-based slack did not match the reserved 22px/34px rail gutter and let wide media slip underneath the transparent seek rail again at mobile widths.
- Clip horizontal overflow of the publication viewport in Continuous mode as a final safety net for constructs width caps cannot reach, such as transformed or absolutely positioned wrappers.

## Regression coverage

The generated Reader EPUB fixture now ships a second oversized wrapper that deliberately avoids figure semantics (`div.oversized-div` with fixed wrapper and pixel widths). The mobile real-browser reliability regression requires both shapes — figure-wrapped and bare-div-wrapped artwork — to render with their right edge at or left of the fixed Continuous seek rail in parent-page coordinates.

The complete v2.6 Chromium, Firefox, WebKit, Chromium Mobile, and WebKit Mobile regression matrix remains required. The reusable v2 publisher must also verify the exact merged `main` SHA, matching Cloudflare production version/commit metadata, public production smoke checks, and the final GitHub release before v2.6.2 is considered promoted.
