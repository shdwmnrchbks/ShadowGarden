# Shadow Garden v2.6.1 — Continuous Reader Rail Hotfix

Shadow Garden v2.6.1 is a focused post-release Reader hotfix for issue #160 and issue #162.

## Fixed

- Keep the Continuous Reader viewport full-width on mobile while reserving the fixed seek rail only inside EPUB publication content sizing.
- Reserve a 22px right-side publication gutter on mobile Continuous layouts and 34px on wider Continuous layouts, so figures and images cannot render underneath the seek rail interaction strip.
- Preserve the v2.6 immersive chrome behavior: hiding the top bar still removes the top chrome gap and does not restore a permanent Reader-side column.
- Keep oversized publication `figure` and `picture` wrappers bounded by the usable EPUB content box.
- Synchronize completion copy and navigation into both the master end page and Continuous-mode clones, including clones mounted before the Reader completion controller initializes.
- Keep every end-page Return to series action bound to the actual current series route, including adult/NSFW series.
- Reuse the canonical Read Again confirmation/reset path when the next volume is already Finished, so it restarts at the beginning instead of reopening at its saved ending position.
- On the last available volume, replace the hidden next-volume action with a Begin the series again action that confirms and resets Volume 1 before reopening it from the beginning.

## Regression coverage

The mobile Real Browser E2E regression now measures artwork in parent-page coordinates and requires its rendered right edge to remain at or left of the fixed Continuous seek rail. The previous v2.6.0 assertion only proved that the artwork stayed inside the EPUB iframe viewport, which allowed the parent-owned rail to overlap otherwise valid iframe geometry.

Reader lifecycle E2E coverage now also mounts a deliberately stale completion clone and verifies that it receives the current catalog volume title, exact series return route, and correct next-volume action. Multi-volume coverage marks the next destination Finished and verifies the Read Again confirmation/reset path, then reaches a final series volume and verifies the restart-to-Volume-1 action.

The complete v2.6 Chromium, Firefox, WebKit, Chromium Mobile, and WebKit Mobile regression matrix remains required. The reusable v2 publisher must also verify the exact merged `main` SHA, matching Cloudflare production version/commit metadata, public production smoke checks, and the final GitHub release before v2.6.1 is considered promoted.
