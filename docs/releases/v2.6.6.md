# Shadow Garden v2.6.6 — Continuous Vertical Sync & Focus Controls

Shadow Garden v2.6.6 fixes two Reader defects reported on desktop browsers, both following the reopened #160 containment work.

## Fixed — vertically long artwork clipped in Continuous mode (desktop)

EPUB.js's continuous layout freezes each section wrapper/frame height at display time. Tall artwork whose rendered height resolves after that settle (late SVG decode; width caps narrowing an image and increasing its height) left the tail beyond the frozen slot where the following section visually cropped it. The adapter now reconciles **every** continuous section idempotently - frame and wrapper heights are rewritten to their document's exact live extent, stale inline offsets are cleared, and natural flow stacking follows real content. Reconciliation re-runs debounced whenever publication media finishes loading plus staggered guaranteed passes after each section render.

## Changed — image focus controls and hints are pointer-aware

- Desktop (fine pointer): plain **wheel zooms** the focused image cursor-anchored, **drag pans** while zoomed, **double-click returns** to the page, and single accidental clicks no longer dismiss the overlay.
- Touch: pinch zoom / drag pan / tap-to-return behavior is unchanged.
- Hints adapt per input class - desktop shows "Scroll to zoom · drag to inspect · double-click to return", touch keeps "Pinch to zoom · drag to inspect · tap again to return" - and **fade out after three seconds** on every platform.

## Regression coverage

The mobile reliability suite gains a Continuous alignment regression asserting no section frame is shorter than its live document extent and no two consecutive sections overlap, with the tall-strip fixture shape reaching the bottom marker in Chromium desktop/mobile suites. Deterministic guards pin the reconciliation loop, wheel-zoom contract, double-click return, adaptive hint wiring, and zero-horizontal-padding continuity from v2.6.5.

The complete v2.6 Chromium, Firefox, WebKit, Chromium Mobile, and WebKit Mobile regression matrix remains required, together with the reusable v2 publisher production gates.
